#A Dashboard of 'Happy Clothing.Co' (imaginary company) using Microsoft Excel.

##Happy Clothing.Co is a clothing company based on California (USA), majorly involve selling daily wears of Women and Men.

Analyzed the 1st quarter's data of the company and solved the following business questions.

1. Trend in the last 13 weeks?
2. How our customers like to buy?
3. How many they buy?
4. Which products are popular?
5. Overall gender split
6. Where do our customers live?
7. How long we take to ship the orders
8. How happy are our customers?

Visually represented the Key Performance Indicators
such as; 
Total Orders,
Total Quantity,
Total Amount,
Avg. Rating,
Avg. Days to Deliver.
And answers for the above questions in the form of dashboard.

##Key Insights-

1. Identified the quantity sold and revenue incurred over time in the course of 1st quarter.
2. Customer's most preferred mode to purchase the products is 'Application'.
3. The majority of customers rated '4 Stars out of 5' to the products.
4. The shipping of almost 40% of orders takes around 1 day to deliver to the customers.
5. Almost 50% of customers are from Los angeles and San diego county in California.
6. The most ordered product by female is 'Jeans', male is 'T-shirts', and others is 'Sneakers'.
